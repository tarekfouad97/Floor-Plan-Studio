Furnish with Claude

Let Claude lay out the room — and wire it.

The planner writes the brief for you: every room at its real size, the doors, the windows and the whole catalogue. You paste it into Claude, paste the answer back, and it places what comes out.

  • 1A layout that respects real clearances. 75 cm to walk through, 90 cm on a route you use often, nothing in a door’s swing, 70 cm to open a wardrobe, 100 cm around a dining table so the chairs pull out.
  • 2The lighting, not just the furniture. Fittings chosen for the size of the room, colour temperature in kelvin, a switch by the door on the handle side, and every light on a named circuit.
  • 3Moods you can switch between. Named scenes that set each fitting to a level — everyday, low evening, one for cooking.
  • 4It tells you when it is wrong. A name that isn’t in the catalogue or a room that isn’t on your plan is reported back, never quietly guessed at.

Sockets, data and supply you place yourself — at worktop height, weatherproof outside — and all of it prints as an electrical schedule next to your shopping list.

Before you start

Questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Open it and use it. There's no account and no trial.

Do I need a Claude account to use it?

No. Drawing and furnishing by hand needs nothing at all. The Claude feature writes you a prompt to paste in wherever you already use Claude, and reads the answer back — the planner itself never talks to a server.

Can it plan the electrics?

Yes. Lights, switches, sockets, data and supply, each on a named circuit, with colour temperature and output per fitting and named scenes that set them to a level. Claude proposes the lighting and the circuits when you use the Furnish step; sockets and the rest you place yourself. It all prints as an electrical schedule.

Where do my plans go?

Into your own browser's storage, and into files you save yourself. Nothing is sent anywhere — the page has no way to upload anything.

How accurate is it?

As accurate as the numbers you give it. Calibrate a photo carefully, or type real tape-measure lengths, and it's exact. Always measure again before buying something expensive.

Can I use it on my phone?

You can view and adjust a plan, and pinch to zoom in 3D. Drawing a flat from scratch is much easier on a laptop.

Can I print it?

Yes — the plan on one page, your shopping list on the next, then a room schedule and an electrical schedule. Or export a PNG.
