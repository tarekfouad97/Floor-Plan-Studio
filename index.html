Before you start

Questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Open it and use it. There's no account and no trial.

Do I need a Claude account to use it?

No. Drawing and furnishing by hand needs nothing at all. The Claude feature writes you a prompt to paste in wherever you already use Claude, and reads the answer back — the planner itself never talks to a server.

Where do my plans go?

Into your own browser's storage, and into files you save yourself. Nothing is sent anywhere — the page has no way to upload anything.

How accurate is it?

As accurate as the numbers you give it. Calibrate a photo carefully, or type real tape-measure lengths, and it's exact. Always measure again before buying something expensive.

Can I use it on my phone?

You can view and adjust a plan, and pinch to zoom in 3D. Drawing a flat from scratch is much easier on a laptop.

Can I print it?

Yes — the plan on one page, your shopping list on the next, then a room schedule and an electrical schedule. Or export a PNG.
