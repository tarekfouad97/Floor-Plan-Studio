Privacy

Who we are

Floor Plan Studio is made by Tarek Fouad, contactable at tare2fo2ad@gmail.com. This policy covers tarekfouad97.github.io/Floor-Plan-Studio.

What the tool stores

Floor plans you draw are saved in your own browser, using local storage on your device. They are not uploaded, and I cannot see them. Clearing your browser data deletes them. Files you export with Save to file live only on your computer.

Images you load as a tracing guide are processed entirely in your browser and never leave your device.

The application is a single HTML file with no network code in it. Once the page has loaded, it makes no requests of any kind.

What is collected

I do not use analytics, tracking scripts, or advertising on this site, and it sets no cookies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As the host, GitHub records standard server logs when a page is requested — including the visitor's IP address — and uses them to run and protect the service. That happens at the hosting layer, before any of my code runs, and I do not have access to those logs. GitHub's handling of them is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU you may ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or erased, and complain to your data protection authority. Because plans stay on your device and no analytics are collected, in practice there is nothing held about you to release or erase. Write to tare2fo2ad@gmail.com with any request.

Changes

Last updated 27 August 2026. Any changes will be posted on this page. If analytics or advertising are ever added, this policy will be updated to say so before they go live.
