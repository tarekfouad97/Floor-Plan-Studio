Privacy policy

Floor Plan Studio is a single web page that runs on your own device. It has no server, no account system and no network code in it. This page explains, in plain terms, what that means for your data — and what would have to change here first if that ever stopped being true.

Last updated: 7 September 2026 Effective: 7 September 2026

The short version

  • ✓We do not collect your name, email, address or any other personal information. There is no form to fill in.
  • ✓Your floor plans never leave your device. There is nowhere for them to go.
  • ✓No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no third-party scripts of any kind.
  • ✓Work in progress is saved in your own browser's local storage, on your device, and you can clear it at any time.

Section 01

Section 02

What this site collects

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, no contact form, no newsletter, no comment box and no upload. The site never asks you for personal information, and it has no server-side code that could receive any.

Specifically, the following are not collected, stored or transmitted by this site:

  • Your name, email address, postal address or phone number
  • Your floor plans, room measurements, furniture choices or any other drawing content
  • Account credentials — there are no accounts
  • Payment details — the site is free and has no payment of any kind
  • Location data, contacts, photos or files, beyond an image you deliberately open as a tracing underlay, which is read in your browser and never sent anywhere
Section 03

What is stored on your own device

So that you do not lose an afternoon's work by closing a tab, the planner saves your current design and a few interface preferences in your browser's local storage. This is a standard browser feature. It is not a cookie, it is never sent in a network request, and it stays on the device you are using.

What is kept there:

  • Your designs — the walls, rooms, furniture, electrical items and notes of each plan you have worked on, so that "My designs" can reopen them
  • Interface preferences — which panels are folded, whether the drawing sheet is set to light or dark, and which sections are open
  • The clipboard — anything you have copied inside the planner, so it survives a reload

You are in control of all of it. Deleting a design inside the planner removes it. Clearing site data for this site in your browser settings removes everything at once, including your designs — so save anything you want to keep to a .json file first. Using the planner in a private or incognito window means nothing is kept once the window closes.

Local storage is per-browser and per-device. A plan saved in Chrome on your laptop is not visible in Safari, on your phone, or to anyone else — including the site operator, who cannot see it at all.
Section 04

Third parties, cookies and trackers

There are none. This site loads no external scripts, stylesheets, web fonts, images, embeds or frames. Every page here is self-contained, which means your browser makes no requests to any domain other than the one hosting the site. You can confirm this yourself in your browser's network inspector.

As a result, this site sets no cookies at all — not necessary ones, not preference ones, not analytics ones — and there is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

There is no analytics of any kind: no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no page-view counter, no heatmaps, no session recording, no error reporting.

There is no advertising on this site, and no advertising or personalisation identifiers are set.

Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub's servers process the technical information every web request necessarily carries — your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser's user-agent string — in order to deliver the page and to protect the service from abuse. This is standard server operation, is not under this site's control, and is not used here for analytics or profiling. GitHub's handling of it is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Section 05

The “Furnish with Claude” feature

This one is worth being precise about, because it is the part people assume sends data somewhere. It does not.

When you use Furnish, the planner writes out a text description of your rooms and the furniture catalogue and places it on your clipboard. You then paste that text into Claude yourself, in your own browser and under your own account, and paste the reply back into the planner. The planner reads the reply, matches the names against its catalogue, and places the furniture.

At no point does Floor Plan Studio contact Claude, Anthropic, or any other service. It contains no API keys and no network-request code whatsoever. The only link to Claude anywhere in the planner is an ordinary Open Claude hyperlink, which you may click or ignore.

If you do choose to paste your room description into Claude, that conversation happens between you and Anthropic under Anthropic's privacy policy and your own account settings there. Nothing about it passes through this site.

Section 06

Files you open and files you save

Opening a plan file or a tracing image uses your browser's ordinary file picker. The file is read into the page's memory on your machine and is never transmitted. Saving — a .json plan, a CSV, a PNG, or a PDF from the print dialog — is an ordinary browser download to a location you choose. Those files are yours; this site keeps no copy and has no way to reach them.

Section 07

Your rights

Data-protection law gives you rights over personal data held about you — the right to know what is held, to get a copy, to have it corrected or erased, to object to its use, and to complain to a regulator.

Those rights apply here in an unusually simple way: this site holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to disclose, correct, export or erase, and no request could return anything. Everything the planner produces is already in your hands — on your device, in files you control.

If you are in the UK or the EEA and believe otherwise, you may contact tare2fo2ad@gmail.com and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

If you are a California resident, the CCPA/CPRA rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or sharing apply in the same way: no personal information is collected, and none has ever been sold or shared for cross-context behavioural advertising or any other purpose.

Section 08

Children

This site is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either, and no age-based profiling of any kind takes place.

Section 09

Changes to this policy

If anything described here changes — if analytics, advertising, cookies or any third-party script is ever added to this site — this page will be rewritten to describe it accurately, and the "last updated" date at the top will change, before the change goes live rather than after. Where the law requires consent, such as for advertising or analytics cookies for visitors in the UK and EEA, that consent will be asked for and recorded before any such script is allowed to load.

Material changes will also be noted on the home page. Continuing to use the site after a change means the updated policy applies to you.
