Plain language privacy

Your plan is yours.

Floor Plan Studio is designed to work on your device. This page explains exactly what that means and what changes if the site later introduces advertising or analytics.

Last updated 30 August 2026

Who runs Floor Plan Studio

Floor Plan Studio is made by Tarek Fouad. Contact: tare2fo2ad@gmail.com. This policy covers tarekfouad97.github.io/Floor-Plan-Studio.

Your floor plans stay on your device

Plans are saved in your own browser using local storage. They are not uploaded, and I cannot see them. Clearing browser data removes saved plans. Files you export live only on your computer.

Images used as tracing guides are processed in your browser and never leave your device.

What is collected today

Floor Plan Studio currently uses no analytics, tracking scripts, advertising, or cookies. After the page loads, the application makes no network requests of its own.

GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, records standard server logs when a page is requested, including IP addresses, to operate and protect the service. That happens before this app runs and is covered by GitHub’s privacy statement.

If advertising is introduced

Advertising is not active today. Before any advertising or analytics is enabled, this policy will be updated with the provider, purpose, data categories, retention details, and how to change consent. Where required, a consent choice will appear before non-essential advertising technology loads.

✦The planner itself will remain usable without an account, and your plan data will not be sold or sent to an ad provider.

Your rights and questions

If you are in the UK or EU, you may ask what data is held about you, request correction or deletion, or complain to your data-protection authority. Because plans stay on your device and no analytics are collected today, there is normally no plan data to provide or erase. Email tare2fo2ad@gmail.com with a request.
